What You'll Learn

  • Master the fundamentals of user interface and user experience design

Course Overview

Welcome to UI/UX Design Fundamentals, where you'll learn everything you need to become a skilled product designer. This course covers both the theory and practical skills needed to create beautiful, functional user interfaces.

Taught by Sarah Johnson, a senior product designer with 10+ years of experience at top tech companies, this course will take you from beginner to job-ready designer. You'll learn industry-standard tools and workflows used by professional design teams.

By the end of this course, you'll have a complete understanding of the design process, from user research to final handoff to developers. You'll also have a portfolio showcasing your design skills to potential employers.
